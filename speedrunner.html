Speedrunner

Pained first love told thru the screens of a video game.

Description

Speedrunner is the coming-of-age story of Mercury, known mostly for her online streaming personality Lulustar. She is climbing the ranks of speedrunning her favorite virtual reality running simulator, Piprun, played on a treadmill in her basement bedroom. She lives with her single mom in New Mexico and their relationship is strained as she approaches college applications.


Mercury only cares about her race time until she meets her new boyfriend Tiger through the game. Unlike the men that watch her stream, Tiger is interested in her and the things she likes. This virtual first love takes Mercury into a new level of escapism from her real-life stress and pressure to meet Tiger in real life builds as their relationship grows.


As she loses herself in first love, Mercury will discover a painful secret that shatters both her virtual and true realities.
