About Me

DIY ✧ Experimentation ✧ Form and Color ✧ Community

Howdy, I'm Shannon Spence! I'm an artist and cartoonist from Hampton, Virginia now based in Brooklyn, NY with my husband and pit-mix, Tater Tot.

My work moves between comics, printmaking, painting, illustration, and performance, unified through playful and LOUD creatures that command attention through expressive color and linework. My background in microbiology and printmaking draws me toward experimentation in color and unusual materials. I love to push a story beyond the medium it starts in!

I have special interest in the ways storytelling can help us to process overwhelming emotions. My current project, Speedrunner, explores first love through the visual language of video games. For my previous comic project P*NK LAB GRL!, I brought my fictional anti-big pharma punk band Mutagen to life through live performance.

I find inner strength through empowering others to self-publish their own comics. In 2022, I founded the Comix Accountability Club, an inclusive community for cartoonist to grow together.

I'm always open to collaborate! I'm especially interested in projects involving building fictional worlds inspired by music, comics, and gaming. Have something in mind? Don't hesitate to reach out:
