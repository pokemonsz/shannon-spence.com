About Me

Howdy, I'm Shannon Spence! I'm a cartoonist, zinemaker, community organizer, and former medical technologist from Hampton, VA. I live with my husband and pittie mix (Tater Tot) in NYC.

I hold a strong belief in the power of comics to move and inspire, and I enjoy creating stories that bring joy and help me to process overwhelming emotions. I started as a woodcut printmaker and fell for comics hard. I love experimentation with unique zine formats, cheap and colorful tools, and new printing methods.

I founded the Comix Accountability Club in 2022. I also started an indie small press called CART Press in 2025 with my zine bestie Maddie Sackett.

Photo by Nicole Millman.

Reach out to me if you'd like to collab, I'm all ears!
